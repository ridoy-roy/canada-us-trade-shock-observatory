# Resume entry

## Canada-U.S. Trade Shock Observatory | Portfolio Project

- Built a reproducible Python pipeline for official U.S. Census import data,
  covering 3,694 HS6-month observations across 164 steel product codes.
- Modeled the 2025 Section 232 tariff timeline, including the March and June
  transition months, and harmonized a 746-line HTS10 product universe to HS6.
- Implemented 108 content-addressed source snapshots, release-hash verification,
  23 automated tests, analytical tables, and publication-ready visualizations.
- Found that the value of Canadian core-steel imports in scope was 36.8% lower
  in 2025 than in 2024 while calculated duty reached $1.07 billion; presented
  the result as descriptive rather than causal.

**Tools:** Python, REST APIs, CSV/JSON, data validation, descriptive analysis,
data visualization, Git, technical documentation
